How the game is played

Settle one faerie in every row, every column and every coloured glade, and never two of them in touching cells — diagonals count. There are no numbers and no arithmetic.

  • Tap a cell to cross it off, and tap again to clear the cross.
  • Double-tap a cell to settle a faerie there.
  • Three dewdrops at the top are your lives. A faerie put somewhere she cannot go costs one. Lose all three and the board starts again; nothing else is lost.

Every board has exactly one answer and every answer can be reasoned out, so you are never asked to guess.

The three helpers

Under the board are Glimmer, which lights the glade worth looking at next; Whisper, which explains the next deduction in words rather than making the move for you; and the Wisp, which drifts across the board and settles on the one cell that is forced. Rewind takes back the last move. All four are free and unlimited.

Questions people ask

Do I need an account, or the internet?

No, and no. There is no sign-in of any kind, and the game plays in airplane mode from the moment it is installed.

How do I change the look?

Settings has two: a soft daylight meadow and a moonlit hedgerow. The same screen holds separate switches for sound, music and haptics, patterns behind the glade colours for anyone who sees colour differently, and reduce motion.

I lost my progress.

Your level, your run of days and your badges live in one file on the device itself and are never sent anywhere, so deleting the app or moving to a new device starts the game over. If progress disappeared while the app stayed installed, write to us with what happened and we will look at it.

How do I erase everything the game has stored?

Settings → Delete my data. It is immediate and it cannot be undone. Deleting the app removes the same file.

Is a board ever unsolvable?

No. Every one of the 3,000 boards was checked solvable by logic alone before it shipped. If one ever looks impossible, Whisper will name the next step.

What languages is it in?

English, in this version.
