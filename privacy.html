Privacy Policy

Faedoku · effective 26 November 2026

Faedoku is a puzzle game published by AJB Games (Amber Jo Bannor).

The short version

Faedoku collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party software development kits in this version of the game. The app makes no network requests at all. It will play with the device in airplane mode from the moment it is installed.

What the app stores, and where

Faedoku saves one file on your own device so that your game is still there when you come back. It holds:

  • which level you have reached, and the board you were part-way through
  • your daily run, your longest run, and the badges you have earned
  • the length of your recent wins, so the game can tell you when you have beaten your own time
  • your settings: the look, music, sound, vibration, colour-blind mode and reduce motion

That file never leaves the device. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone else. Nothing in it identifies you: there is no name, no email address, no device identifier and no advertising identifier in it, because the game never asks for any of those things.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete my data erases that file and returns the game to the state it was in when you first installed it. It is immediate and it cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes the file, in the ordinary way iOS and Android remove an app's own storage.

Because nothing is ever sent to us, there is nothing held about you anywhere else to request, correct or delete. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR we are not a controller of any personal data through this app; under the CCPA we collect no personal information and sell none.

Children

Faedoku's target audience is adults, and it is not directed to children. It contains no advertising, no purchases and no way to contact another person. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from anyone of any age, which is what COPPA, the GDPR's Article 8 and Google Play's Families policy are concerned with.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It uses the device's vibration motor for haptic feedback, which needs no permission on iOS and is declared on Android as VIBRATE; vibration can be turned off in Settings.

Changes

If a future version of Faedoku adds advertising, analytics or anything else that collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent in the app before any such thing starts. That is not what this version does.
